## 🚨 ESCALATION PROTOCOL - FASE 5

### Critical Escalation Triggers:

**Trigger 1: Asesor Cannot Access Key Documents or Facilities**
- Response: Immediate coordination with appropriate office
- Action: Provide access within 1 hour or escalate to Rektor
- Prevention: Document room fully prepared before visit

**Trigger 2: Staff Member Provides Inaccurate Information to Asesor**
- Response: Quickly clarify with responsible office and asesor
- Action: Provide correction to asesor (preferably same day)
- Prevention: Staff briefing covered likely scenarios

**Trigger 3: Critical Facility or Equipment Failure During Visit**
- Response: Immediate notification to asesor of issue
- Action: Explain issue and remediation plans
- Transparency: Don't hide or minimize problems

**Trigger 4: Asesor Indicates Major Accreditation Concerns**
- Response: Listen carefully, take detailed notes
- Action: Ask clarifying questions professionally
- Follow-up: Brief Rektor immediately after visit closes

**Trigger 5: Formal Report Includes Unexpected Critical Findings**
- Response: Convene leadership immediately upon report receipt
- Action: Analyze findings, develop immediate response plan
- Communication: Keep stakeholders informed
