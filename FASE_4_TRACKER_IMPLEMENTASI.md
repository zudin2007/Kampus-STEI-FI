## 🚨 ESCALATION PROTOCOL - FASE 4

**Trigger 1: Leadership Review Feedback Cannot Be Resolved**
- Response: Koordinator + LPM Director + responsible WK meet immediately
- Decision: Prioritize critical issues, defer minor issues if necessary
- Escalation: Brief Rektor on any content/evidence concerns

**Trigger 2: BAN-PT Submission Portal Technical Issues**
- Response: Contact BAN-PT technical support immediately
- Alternative: Paper submission if digital fails (unlikely but contingency)
- Timeline: Must resolve within 24 hours to meet Nov 30 deadline

**Trigger 3: Rektor Unavailable for Sign-Off (Before Nov 11)**
- Response: Prepare document for immediate sign-off upon return
- Contingency: Wakil Rektor authorized to sign (if delegation documented)
- Timeline: Critical - affects entire submission schedule

**Trigger 4: Quality Assessment Falls Below 85/100**
- Response: Immediate revision sprint (Thu-Fri of W11)
- Action: LPM identifies specific sections needing improvement
- Owner: Responsible WK coordinates writers/revisers
- Escalation: Brief Rektor on any quality concerns

**Trigger 5: Major Issues Discovered Post-Submission**
- Response: Document the issue and mitigation
- Escalation: Brief Rektor and LPM Director
- Action: If critical, prepare amendment request to BAN-PT
