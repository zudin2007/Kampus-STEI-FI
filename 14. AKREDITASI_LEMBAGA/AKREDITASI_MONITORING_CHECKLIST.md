## 🚨 ESCALATION PROTOCOL

**If any of 8 Critical Must-Haves is AT RISK:**

1. **Immediate Action** (24 hours):
   - [ ] Alert Koordinator & Pimpinan
   - [ ] Emergency team meeting
   - [ ] Root cause analysis
   - [ ] Contingency plan prepared

2. **Escalation to Leadership** (48 hours):
   - [ ] Present issue & mitigation to Rektor
   - [ ] Request decision/approval
   - [ ] Resource reallocation if needed

3. **Follow-up** (Daily until resolved):
   - [ ] Daily status update
   - [ ] Accelerated timeline untuk closure
