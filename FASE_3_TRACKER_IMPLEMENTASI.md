## 🎯 QUALITY RUBRIC (Detailed Scoring Guide)

### **Content Quality (30 points)**

**27-30 points: Excellent**
- Comprehensive coverage of all required elements
- Clear alignment with BAN-PT standards
- Thoughtful analysis of institutional strengths & areas for improvement
- Balanced, honest assessment

**24-26 points: Good**
- Most elements covered, 1-2 minor gaps
- Generally aligned with standards
- Some analysis of strengths & improvements
- Mostly balanced perspective

**20-23 points: Satisfactory**
- Basic coverage of key elements
- Some gaps or shallow areas
- Limited analysis; mostly descriptive
- Some balance but favors strengths over improvements

**15-19 points: Needs Improvement**
- Missing key elements
- Significant gaps
- Minimal analysis; very descriptive
- Unbalanced (too rosy or too critical)

**0-14 points: Insufficient**
- Incomplete coverage
- Major gaps in content
- No analysis
- Poorly organized

---

### **Data Integration (30 points)**

**27-30 points: Excellent**
- Data seamlessly integrated throughout narrative
- Every major claim supported by specific data
- Clear evidence of all 55+ KPI indicators used
- Strong data × narrative connection

**24-26 points: Good**
- Data well-integrated in most sections
- Most claims supported by data
- Evidence of most key indicators used
- Good data × narrative connection

**20-23 points: Satisfactory**
- Data integrated into some sections
- Basic claims supported by data
- Some indicators used
- Data × narrative connection present but could be stronger

**15-19 points: Needs Improvement**
- Limited data integration
- Few claims explicitly supported by data
- Minimal indicator usage
- Weak data × narrative connection

**0-14 points: Insufficient**
- Little to no data integration
- Unsupported claims
- Indicators not referenced
- No data × narrative connection

---

### **Analysis Quality (20 points)**

**18-20 points: Excellent**
- Critical thinking evident (not just description)
- Insightful observations about strengths & gaps
- Clear cause-effect reasoning
- Thoughtful suggestions for improvement

**16-17 points: Good**
- Some critical thinking
- Observations show understanding
- Logic mostly clear
- Suggestions reasonable

**14-15 points: Satisfactory**
- Minimal critical thinking
- Mostly descriptive with some analysis
- Logic adequate
- Suggestions present but basic

**10-13 points: Needs Improvement**
- Very limited critical thinking
- Mostly descriptive
- Logic sometimes unclear
- Weak improvement suggestions

**0-9 points: Insufficient**
- No analysis, pure description
- Logic flawed or absent
- No suggestions for improvement

---

### **Format & Clarity (15 points)**

**14-15 points: Excellent**
- Professional formatting (margins, fonts, spacing)
- Clear structure & logical flow
- Easy to read & understand
- Citations in proper format

**12-13 points: Good**
- Mostly professional formatting (1-2 minor issues)
- Clear structure
- Generally easy to read
- Mostly proper citations

**11 points: Satisfactory**
- Adequate formatting (some inconsistencies)
- Adequate structure
- Readable with effort
- Inconsistent citations

**8-10 points: Needs Improvement**
- Poor formatting (distracting)
- Unclear structure
- Difficult to read
- Citation issues

**0-7 points: Insufficient**
- Very poor formatting
- Confusing structure
- Hard to read
- Missing citations

---

### **Writing Quality (5 points)**

**5 points: Excellent**
- No grammar/spelling errors
- Professional tone throughout
- Clear, concise language
- Natural flow

**4 points: Good**
- Few errors (1-2 typos)
- Professional tone mostly
- Clear language
- Good flow

**3 points: Satisfactory**
- Several errors (not distracting)
- Mostly professional tone
- Adequate clarity
- Adequate flow

**2 points: Needs Improvement**
- Many errors (distracting)
- Inconsistent tone
