## 5️⃣ CROSS-UNIT COORDINATION TIPS

### **Where Units Share Responsibility**

**Standar 4 (Students):**
- **WK1 owns:** Enrollment data, graduation rates, academic advising
- **WK3 owns:** Student support services, student satisfaction
- **Coordination:** Share enrollment numbers. WK3 gets student support satisfaction.

**Standar 5 (Faculty):**
- **WK1 owns:** Faculty data, credentials, teaching load
- **LPPM owns:** Research output and productivity
- **Coordination:** Make sure publication lists are consistent between sources.

**Standar 6 (Facilities):**
- **WK2 PRIMARY:** All facility data, inventory, photos, condition assessment, maintenance, IT infrastructure
- **WK3 SUPPORT:** Student-facing facilities perspective, student satisfaction, student services context
- **Coordination:** WK3 provides input to WK2's comprehensive facility assessment (student needs, satisfaction)

**Standar 7 (Research):**
- **LPPM owns:** Research output, funding, publications, community service
- **WK1 owns:** Student research participation, faculty research context
- **Coordination:** WK1 provides student research numbers. LPPM provides detailed project data.

### **Weekly Coordination Meeting (Fridays 15:00)**

Everyone meets together at 15:00 Friday to:
1. Report progress on own Standar
2. Identify cross-unit issues (e.g., enrollment numbers conflict)
3. Resolve conflicts immediately
4. Plan next week's activities
5. Escalate blockers to Rektor if needed

**Duration:** 45 minutes to 1 hour  
**Attendance:** All WK heads + LPPM + LPM Director + Koordinator  
**Format:** Each unit: 5-10 min update, Q&A, decisions

### **Data Conflict Resolution**

**When numbers don't match** (e.g., enrollment in 2 places):

Step 1: Identify source documents
Step 2: Determine which is "truth source" (official record)
Step 3: Align other data to truth source
Step 4: Document decision & reason
Step 5: LPM approves resolved data
Step 6: Move forward

**Example:** "Enrollment from Academic DB = 547 students. HR shows 545. Academic DB is truth source (official enrollment). HR shows some withdrawals not yet processed. Resolved: Use 547."

### **Escalation Procedure**

**Level 1 (WK to WK coordination):**
"WK1 and WK3, we have conflict on Standar 4 numbers. Can you sync this by tomorrow?"
→ Usually resolved same day

**Level 2 (Through Koordinator):**
"I can't resolve this. Koordinator, can you facilitate meeting?"
→ Resolved within 24-48 hours

**Level 3 (To Rektor):**
"This requires decision from leadership (e.g., which data source is official?)."
→ Rektor decides, problem solved

**Don't skip levels.** Always try WK-to-WK first, then Koordinator, then Rektor.

### **Communication Protocol**

**Monday through Friday:**
- Daily check-ins: Quick Whatsapp/email (5 min)
- 48-hour response rule: Don't leave questions hanging
- Problem flag: Report issues immediately, don't wait
- Friday meeting: Major updates + issues resolution

**Weekend:** No work expected (take a break!)

**Before Monday Morning:**
- Submit any pending data
- Prepare for Monday status check
