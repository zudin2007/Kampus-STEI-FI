## 🚨 ESCALATION PROTOCOL

### **CRITICAL (24-hour response)**
Examples: Cash shortage, payroll delay, major facility breakdown, audit finding

**Action:**
1. Notify WK2 immediately
2. WK2 briefs Wakil Rektor
3. Action plan within 4 hours
4. Daily updates until resolved

### **HIGH PRIORITY (48-hour response)**
Examples: Budget overrun, revenue shortfall, facility issue, HR concern

**Action:**
1. Notify Kepala unit immediately
2. WK2 reviews & approves action
3. Execute corrective action
4. Update status daily

### **ROUTINE (Weekly review)**
Examples: Normal reconciliations, schedule maintenance, regular reports

**Action:**
1. Handle via normal weekly process
2. Include in Friday report
3. Track to resolution
