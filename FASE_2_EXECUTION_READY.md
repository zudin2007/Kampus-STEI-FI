   - Rektor must actively support WK1 on Standar 1-2
   - Rektor must attend key meetings
   - Rektor must model "data quality matters" message

2. **Unit Head Ownership**
   - Each WK/LPPM must personally oversee data collection
   - Not delegating completely (spot-check quality yourself)
   - Daily status updates to team

3. **Quality Over Speed**
   - ≥95% accuracy is NON-NEGOTIABLE

   - Better to submit 30% of good data than 50% of bad

4. **LPM Quality Gate**
   - LPM must enforce ≥95% accuracy standard
   - Cannot approve bad data just because deadline approaching
   - Must catch quality issues early (W2), not late (W4)

5. **Communication Discipline**
   - Daily updates required (no silent struggles)
   - Problems flagged immediately (not hidden)
   - Weekly coordination (Friday 15:00, mandatory)
