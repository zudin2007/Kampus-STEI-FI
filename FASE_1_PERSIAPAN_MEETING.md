### **MEETING AGENDA & TALKING POINTS**

**KICK-OFF MEETING: PERSIAPAN AKREDITASI LEMBAGA**
**Duration: 60 minutes | Time: 08:00-09:00**

```
08:00-08:05 | WELCOME & OPENING (Rektor Lead)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Rektor Remarks (Talking Points):
├─ Welcome to all participants
├─ Importance of accreditation to institution future
├─ Acknowledgment of team effort required
├─ Confidence in institution's readiness
└─ Commitment to support the process

Key Message: "Akreditasi adalah investasi untuk masa depan institusi.
             Dengan dukungan penuh kami semua, kami siap mencapai hasil terbaik."

Tone: Formal, encouraging, visionary
Duration: 5 minutes


08:05-08:15 | AKREDITASI OVERVIEW & TIMELINE (Koordinator Lead)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Content to Cover:
├─ What is BAN-PT accreditation? (definition & importance)
├─ 5 phase timeline overview:
│  ├─ Phase 1 (W1-2): Persiapan & setup
│  ├─ Phase 2 (W2-4): Pengumpulan data
│  ├─ Phase 3 (W5-10): Penyusunan SER
│  ├─ Phase 4 (W11-13): Finalisasi & submission
│  └─ Phase 5 (W14-20): Visitasi & post-visitasi
├─ Critical dates:
│  ├─ W13 (Nov 30): SER SUBMISSION DEADLINE
│  └─ W15-18 (Dec 8-31): Asesor visitasi
└─ Success requirements (what needs to be accomplished)

Visual Aids:
├─ Timeline slide (showing all 5 phases)
├─ 7 Standar overview slide
├─ Critical path slide (what blocks what)
└─ Success criteria slide

Key Takeaway: Institution has 19 weeks to prepare comprehensive accreditation
Duration: 10 minutes


08:15-08:25 | STRATEGY & KEY MESSAGES (Koordinator Lead)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Strategy Pillars:
├─ Data-Driven: All decisions based on facts & evidence
├─ Honest Assessment: Acknowledge strengths AND areas for improvement
├─ Comprehensive: Cover all 7 standards thoroughly
├─ Quality Focus: Target ≥85/100 for SER
└─ Professional: Demonstrate institutional excellence

Key Messages to Communicate:
1. "Akreditasi bukan hanya pemenuhan regulasi, tapi roadmap improvement"
2. "Data yang akurat & lengkap adalah kunci sukses kami"
3. "Transparansi & kejujuran akan dihargai asesor"
4. "Setiap unit punya peran krusial dalam kesuksesan ini"

Approach:
├─ Inclusive: All voices heard
├─ Transparent: No surprises, clear communication
├─ Supportive: Resources & coaching available
└─ Accountable: Clear responsibilities & timelines

Expected Outcome: Full leadership buy-in & commitment
Duration: 10 minutes


08:25-08:35 | TEAM STRUCTURE & ROLES (Koordinator Lead)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Organizational Structure:

            ┌─────────────────────────────┐
            │    STEERING COMMITTEE       │
            │  (Rektor + WK1/2/3 Lead)   │
            └──────────────┬──────────────┘
                           │
    ┌──────────────┬───────┼────────┬──────────────┐
    │              │               │              │
┌─────────┐  ┌─────────┐  ┌─────────┐  ┌──────────┐
│  WK1    │  │  WK2    │  │  WK3    │  │   LPM    │
│Academic │  │Finance/ │  │Facilities  │ Quality  │
│         │  │  Admin  │  │ Student    │Assurance │
└─────────┘  └─────────┘  └─────────┘  └──────────┘

Roles & Responsibilities:

1. Rektor (Leadership Sponsor)
   └─ Provide strategic direction & support
   └─ Remove blockers at institutional level
   └─ Sign-off on final SER

2. Koordinator Akreditasi (Project Manager)
   └─ Overall coordination & timeline management
   └─ Data collection orchestration
   └─ Communication hub between all units
   └─ LPM coordination
   └─ SER assembly & submission

3. WK1 - Bidang Akademik (Academic Lead)
   └─ Responsible for: Standar 1-2 (Visi/Governance), Standar 5 (Faculty)
   └─ Data: Visi-misi, strategic plans, governance, faculty credentials
   └─ Lead SER writers for Standar 1-2, 4-5

4. WK2 - Bidang Keuangan & Administrasi (Finance/Admin Lead)
   └─ Responsible for: Standar 3 (Resources), Standar 6 (Facilities)
   └─ Data: Budget, revenue, assets, maintenance
   └─ Lead SER writer for Standar 3, 6

5. WK3 - Bidang Sarana & Kemahasiswaan (Facilities/Student Lead)
   └─ Responsible for: Standar 4 (Students), Standar 6 (Facilities)
   └─ Data: Student data, enrollment, facilities, student services
   └─ Lead SER writer for Standar 4, 6

6. LPM Director (Quality Assurance)
   └─ Data validation (≥95% accuracy)
   └─ Evidence completeness review (100%)
   └─ SER quality assessment (≥85/100)
   └─ Weekly quality reviews
   └─ Final sign-off before submission

7. Kaprodi (Academic Program Coordinators)
   └─ Support WK1 in data collection
   └─ Program-specific documentation
   └─ Student outcome data
   └─ Faculty curriculum alignment

8. Department Heads (Kepala Unit)
   └─ Collect & organize unit-specific data
   └─ Support WK data gathering
   └─ Respond to data quality issues
   └─ Provide staff support during visitasi

Communication Channels:
├─ Weekly meetings (Tue 10:00, Thu 14:00, Fri 15:00)
├─ Email updates (daily if needed)
├─ Escalation: WK → Koordinator → WR/Rektor
└─ Emergency contact: Koordinator (phone/WhatsApp)

Accountability:
├─ Each WK owns their Standar delivery
├─ LPM owns quality gates
├─ Koordinator owns overall timeline
├─ Rektor ultimate accountability

Duration: 10 minutes


08:35-08:45 | Q&A & COMMITMENT (Rektor Facilitate)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Q&A Process:
├─ Rektor invites questions
├─ Koordinator takes notes (write on flipchart)
├─ Answers provided immediately or scheduled follow-up
└─ Document all Q&A for later reference

Anticipated Questions & Answers:

Q: "Berapa banyak beban kerja tambahan untuk unit kami?"
A: "Beban akan terkonsentrasi di W2-4 (data collection). Kami provide templates
   & support. Estimates: 5-10 jam per minggu untuk lead person, 20 jam total
   untuk unit. Support team tersedia untuk membantu."

Q: "Bagaimana jika kita tidak siap dengan semua data?"
A: "LPM & Koordinator akan identify gaps early. Kami punya recovery plan.
   Prioritas: quality > completeness. Missing data bisa dijelaskan dalam SER
   dengan action plan untuk improvement."

Q: "Apa yang terjadi kalau kami tidak accredited?"
A: "Fokus kita adalah accredited. Dengan persiapan ini, target adalah B atau
   lebih tinggi. Jika ada feedback dari asesor, kami akan develop improvement
   plan. Tapi ini bukan fokus kita sekarang."

Q: "Siapa yang write SER?"
A: "Setiap WK lead akan write Standar mereka. Koordinator coordinate assembly.
   Professional editor akan review quality. Kami support dengan coaching."

Commitment Request:
├─ "Kami meminta komitmen penuh dari semua unit"
├─ "Timeline adalah mandatory - tidak ada extension untuk W13 submission"
├─ "Quality adalah prioritas - lebih baik berkualitas daripada cepat"
└─ "Komunikasi terbuka & transparan adalah kunci sukses"

Rektor Closing Commitment Statement:
"Saya berkomitmen penuh untuk mendukung proses akreditasi ini. Kami akan
alokasikan resources yang dibutuhkan dan remove semua blockers. Mari kita
bersama-sama menghasilkan karya yang excellent dan membanggakan institusi."

Duration: 10 minutes


08:45-09:00 | NEXT STEPS & CLOSING (Koordinator Lead)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Navigator Distribution:
├─ Printed copies available today
├─ Email copies to be sent tomorrow
├─ Navigation guide to all materials
└─ How to find information & escalate questions

Communication Plan:

Closing Remarks:
"Terima kasih atas komitmen & partisipasi anda. Ini adalah langkah
penting untuk masa depan institusi. Mari kita move forward dengan
momentum & semangat tinggi. Sampai jumpa di meeting berikutnya."

Duration: 15 minutes (flexible for additional Q&A)
```
