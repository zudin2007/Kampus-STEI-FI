## 🚀 GETTING STARTED (UNTUK YANG BARU)

### **Step 1: UNDERSTAND THE BIG PICTURE (30 menit)**
1. Baca: `INTEGRASI_AKREDITASI_SISTEM.md` (ini file, overview)
2. Baca: `14/AKREDITASI_OVERVIEW.md` (strategi)

### **Step 2: UNDERSTAND YOUR ROLE (15 menit)**
- Cari role Anda di section "FIND BY ROLE" di atas
- Baca primary resources yang disebutkan

### **Step 3: FIND YOUR PHASE (20 menit)**
- Lihat bagian "FIND BY PHASE"
- Baca core documents untuk fase current

### **Step 4: ACCESS TOOLS & TEMPLATES (Ongoing)**
- Gunakan navigator ini untuk quick reference
- Bookmark folder yang sering diakses
- Ask questions ke Koordinator jika bingung

### **Step 5: PARTICIPATE IN COORDINATION (Weekly)**
- Week 1-4: Weekly sync meeting (Tuesday 10:00-11:00)
- After W4: Bi-weekly deep review (Thursday 14:00-15:00)
- Monthly: Status review (Last Friday 09:00-10:00)
