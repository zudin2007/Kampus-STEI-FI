# 📊 LPM AKREDITASI MONITORING - QUALITY ASSURANCE TRACKING

**Daily/Weekly/Monthly monitoring checklist untuk LPM dalam memastikan kualitas persiapan akreditasi BAN-PT**

---

## 📋 OVERVIEW

**LPM Role:** Quality Assurance untuk persiapan akreditasi institusional BAN-PT

**Primary Responsibilities:**
1. Data validation & accuracy audit
2. Evidence completeness verification
3. SER quality review
4. BAN-PT compliance checking
5. Continuous monitoring & feedback
6. Final approval sebelum submission

## ☎️ ESCALATION CRITERIA

**Escalate to Koordinator IMMEDIATELY if:**

🔴 **CRITICAL (24 hours):**
- Data accuracy <85%
- Evidence completeness <90%
- BAN-PT compliance <80%
- Quality score decline >10 points
- Timeline risk of delay

🟡 **HIGH PRIORITY (48 hours):**
- Quality score concern (70-80)
- SER revision delay
- Resource constraint
- Team capacity issue

🟢 **ROUTINE (Next meeting):**
- Minor quality issues
- Process improvement suggestions
- Status updates
