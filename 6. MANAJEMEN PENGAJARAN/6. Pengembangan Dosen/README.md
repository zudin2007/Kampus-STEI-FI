# 6. PENGEMBANGAN DOSEN

## Deskripsi

Folder ini mengelola pengembangan kompetensi dosen dalam bidang pembelajaran, meliputi:
- Program pelatihan & workshop pengajaran
- Mentoring & coaching untuk dosen
- Sertifikasi & credential pengajaran
- Refleksi pembelajaran & continuous improvement
- Recognition & incentive untuk dosen excellent

## File-File Utama

- `TEMPLATE_LEARNING_PLAN_DOSEN.md` - Template individual learning plan
- `TEMPLATE_WORKSHOP_PENGAJARAN.md` - Template workshop/training
- `GUIDE_MENTORING_DOSEN.md` - Panduan mentoring dosen
- `RUBRIK_EVALUASI_DOSEN.md` - Rubrik evaluasi kinerja dosen
- `TEMPLATE_REFLEKSI_PEMBELAJARAN.md` - Template refleksi pembelajaran

## Kompetensi Dosen yang Dikembangkan

### A. Content Knowledge & Expertise
- [ ] Penguasaan mendalam atas mata kuliah yang diampu
- [ ] Update dengan perkembangan terbaru di bidang
- [ ] Kemampuan integrate teori & praktik

### B. Pedagogical Knowledge
- [ ] Memahami cara mahasiswa belajar
- [ ] Desain pembelajaran yang effective
- [ ] Berbagai metode pembelajaran
- [ ] Assessment & penilaian

### C. Professional Skills
- [ ] Communication & presentation
- [ ] Time management & organization
- [ ] Technology integration
- [ ] Collaboration & teamwork

### D. Soft Skills
- [ ] Empathy & student care
- [ ] Coaching & mentoring
- [ ] Critical thinking & problem solving
- [ ] Continuous learning mindset

## Program Pengembangan Dosen

### A. Workshop & Training
**Frekuensi:** Min. 2 workshop per tahun per dosen

**Topik Yang Mungkin:**
- Innovative Teaching Methods (Flipped Classroom, PBL, Case-Based Learning)
- Technology Integration in Teaching (LMS, Video, Online Tools)
- Assessment & Evaluation Design
- Student-Centered Learning
- Communication & Presentation Skills
- Mentoring & Coaching Skills
- Curriculum Alignment
- Learning Outcomes Design

**Format:**
- Full-day workshop (8 jam)
- Half-day workshop (4 jam)
- Online course/webinar
- Self-paced learning modules

### B. Mentoring & Coaching
**Target:** Setiap dosen junior/new dosen punya mentor

**Mentor:**
- Dosen senior/experienced (min. 5 tahun)
- Punya track record teaching excellence
- Willing to share & develop others

**Struktur Mentoring:**
- Monthly one-on-one meeting
- Classroom observation & feedback
- RPS & material review
- Career guidance & planning

**Duration:** Min. 1 tahun untuk new dosen, ongoing untuk junior dosen

### C. Sertifikasi & Credential
**Jenis Sertifikasi:**
- Certified Effective Teacher (internal program)
- Workshop certificates (external providers)
- Professional certifications (e.g., TEFL untuk bahasa)
- Degree advancement (Master's, PhD dalam bidang)

**Benefit of Sertifikasi:**
- Recognition
- Career advancement
- Increased motivation
- Evidence of competency

### D. Refleksi Pembelajaran Berkelanjutan
**Prosedur:**
1. After each semester: dosen tulis refleksi pembelajaran
2. Identify: Apa yang berhasil? Apa yang perlu diperbaiki?
3. Set goals untuk semester depan
4. Share dengan Kaprodi & mentor
5. Integrate ke dalam teaching improvement plan

**Tools:** Refleksi Journal / Portfolio

## Learning Plan Individual

### Komponen
1. **Current State Analysis**
   - Kekuatan dosen (apa yang sudah baik)
   - Area untuk improvement (gap analysis)
   - Aspirasi & goals

2. **Development Goals**
   - SMART goals untuk 1 tahun ke depan
   - Prioritize (mana yang paling penting)

3. **Development Activities**
   - Training/workshop yang akan diikuti
   - Mentoring yang akan didapat
   - Reading/self-study yang akan dilakukan
   - Project/initiative yang akan dijalankan

4. **Timeline & Milestones**
   - Kapan akan mulai/selesai
   - Checkpoint untuk monitor progress

5. **Success Metrics**
   - Bagaimana akan measure success
   - Evidence yang akan dikumpulkan

6. **Resources & Support**
   - Apa resource yang dibutuhkan
   - Support dari institusi/Kaprodi

## Evaluasi & Recognition

### A. Performance Evaluation
**Frekuensi:** Tahunan

**Kriteria Evaluasi:**
- Teaching effectiveness (student evaluation score)
- Learning outcomes achievement
- Innovation & improvement
- Professional development
- Collaboration & teamwork

**Output:** Teaching evaluation report + feedback

### B. Recognition Program
**Bentuk Recognition:**
- Best Teacher Award (tahunan)
- Teaching Excellence Certificate
- Bonus/incentive
- Promotion opportunity
- Leadership role (mentor, committee)

**Selection Criteria:**
- High student evaluation score (≥4/5)
- High LO achievement (≥85%)
- Innovation dalam pembelajaran
- Mentoring contribution
- Peer recognition

## Budget & Timeline

### Annual Teaching Development Budget
- Per dosen: 5-10 juta (estimated untuk workshop, conference, certification)
- Mentor incentive: 2-3 juta/tahun
- Award: 5-10 juta untuk best teacher award

### Timeline
- **July-August:** Planning untuk tahun ajaran (set goals, arrange mentoring)
- **September onwards:** Implementation workshop & training
- **Semester-end:** Evaluation & refleksi

| Workshop Attendance | ≥80% | Attendance tracking |
| Learning Plan Completion | ≥90% | % dosen dengan approved LP |

| Innovation Adoption | ≥30% dosen | # dosen trying innovations |

## Best Practices

1. **Make it Mandatory & Valued:** Teaching development bukan optional, tapi expectation & valued
2. **Individual Needs:** Tailor program ke individual needs & aspirations
3. **Mentoring Model:** Strong mentoring program untuk junior/new dosen
4. **Continuous:** Ongoing, bukan one-time event
5. **Support:** Provide resources, time, & incentive
6. **Recognition:** Publicly recognize excellence dalam teaching

## Tantangan & Solusi

| Tantangan | Solusi |
|-----------|--------|
| Dosen tidak motivated | Show benefit, recognize excellence, create culture |
| Time constraint | Flexible schedule, online options, release time |
| Limited budget | Use internal resources, grant, online free resources |
| Resistance to change | Involve dosen, show evidence, peer influence |

## Integration dengan Sistem Lain

- **HR System:** Teaching development integrate dengan career advancement
- **Performance Evaluation:** Teaching effectiveness jadi bagian dari evaluation
- **Compensation:** Excellence dalam teaching bisa affect bonus/increment
- **Promotion:** Teaching excellence adalah criteria untuk promotion
